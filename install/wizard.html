Connect Chrome

What it is
A small add-on for Google Chrome, called an extension.
Why you want it
Jarvis can do the clicking in the websites you already use.
How it helps
Ask it to look up a customer, fill in a web form, or pull numbers from a report. It works in your own sign-ins.

Three clicks, in order. Each one opens a small window of its own.

  1. Use your work email. If the window stays open after you sign in, close it.
  2. Click Add to Chrome in the window that opens. If Chrome already asked you to turn on "Claude", click Enable instead. This page ticks it once Chrome has it. If the window stays open, close it.
  3. Adding it is not enough: Claude in Chrome only works once you sign in to it. Sign in with the same work email in the window that opens, and close it. Then click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, pin Claude, and click it: if it asks you to sign in, do that too. It is ready when it shows a box to type in.

If Claude offers to import cookies from your browser: that copies your website sign-ins into the browser built into the Claude app. You can skip it, because Claude in Chrome already uses your Chrome sign-ins. If you do want it, tick only the site you are working on and untick the rest.

Publish websites and apps

Jarvis can build things like a booking form, a price calculator or a simple website, and put them online with a link you can share. Two accounts make that work, and both are free to start.

  1. Vercel is the service that puts your pages on the internet. A Vercel window opens: sign up or sign in (Continue with Google or GitHub is quickest), then click Allow. This page ticks it when it is done.
  2. Supabase is a secure online place to save what your pages collect, like form answers or bookings. A Supabase window opens: sign up or sign in, click Generate new token (a private password for this setup), give it any name, and copy it. Paste it here.

Both are free to start. Vercel's free plan is for personal sites; a site for a business belongs on Vercel Pro (about $20 a month). Supabase's free plan allows business use, with two projects that pause after a week without visits.

You can skip this: the first time you ask Jarvis to publish something, it walks you through signing in.
