# /get-help - Let Someone See Your Screen

This uses Chrome Remote Desktop, Google's free screen-sharing tool. Nobody can connect unless the
person here creates a code, gives it out, and clicks Share.

## Instructions

### 1. Check who is helping
Ask who they are getting help from. If someone called, texted or emailed them first and asked for
remote access, tell them to stop: that is how remote-access scams work. Have them contact their
support person themselves, using the contact in `START-HERE.md`.

Wait for their answer before going on. If someone contacted them first, or they are not sure who
it is, stop here and do not open the page.

### 2. Open the page
Open `https://remotedesktop.google.com/support` in Google Chrome.
- Mac: `open -a "Google Chrome" "https://remotedesktop.google.com/support"`
- Windows: `powershell -NoProfile -Command "Start-Process chrome 'https://remotedesktop.google.com/support'"`

If it does not open, ask them to open Google Chrome and go to that address.

### 3. Tell them what to do
1. If **Get Support** shows **Download**, click it and follow Google's steps. The computer may ask
   for its password or to approve a security setting.
2. Click **Generate Code** and read the code to your support person. It works one time.
3. When a box shows their email address, check it is the right person, then click **Share**.
4. Click **Stop Sharing** when you are done.

## Rules
- Never click Download, Generate Code or Share for them.
- Never ask for the code in this chat, and never send it anywhere. If they paste it here, tell them
  to generate a new one.
- While sharing, the helper can see and use everything on this computer. Suggest closing anything
  private first.
